Privacy Policy

Strategic Growth — operated by Daniel Ortiz · Last updated 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Strategic Growth, a growth-consulting business operated by Daniel Ortiz ("Strategic Growth", "we", "us"), collects and uses the information you provide when you contact us through our Meta (Facebook/Instagram) lead form or through this website (strategicgrowthhq.vercel.app). It applies to prospective clients considering our $497 Growth Audit or $1,900 Growth Pilot.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Strategic Growth is the business responsible for information you submit to us. For any privacy question or request, contact us at strategicgrowth.biz@outlook.com.

2. Information we collect through the Meta lead form

When you submit our Meta Instant Form, we receive only the answers you provide in that form. Those categories are:

  • Your name
  • Business email address
  • Business telephone number
  • Business name
  • Business website (if you choose to provide one)
  • Service category that best describes your business
  • The market or location you serve
  • Your principal growth or operational bottleneck
  • Your intended start timeframe or urgency
  • Your role in the decision (decision-maker, influencer, or researching for someone else)
  • Your interest in the $497 Growth Audit or the $1,900 Growth Pilot

We ask only for information needed to assess whether we can help and to follow up about our services. Please do not submit sensitive personal information (such as government identifiers, health, or financial-account details); we do not request it.

Meta's role versus ours. The lead form is hosted on Meta's platform. When you submit it, Meta collects and processes your responses on its own platform under Meta's Privacy Policy, and then makes your submitted answers available to us. Strategic Growth's use of your information — described in this policy — begins once we retrieve those answers from Meta. We do not control Meta's own collection or use of data on its platform.

3. How we use your information

  • To evaluate whether your business is a fit for a Growth Audit or Growth Pilot;
  • To contact you about your enquiry and our services;
  • To prepare for and schedule an introductory conversation if you request one;
  • To keep accurate records of enquiries and our own service quality.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising to third parties.

4. Website information

If you use this website, we may record standard, largely anonymous technical information (such as pages viewed, referring URLs, and campaign attribution parameters like UTM codes) to understand and improve our own site and acquisition channels. If you choose to book a call, the name and email you enter are passed to our scheduling provider to set up that booking.

5. Service providers we share with

We share information only with providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Meta Platforms — hosts the lead form and delivers your submitted answers to us;
  • Our scheduling provider — used only if you choose to book a call, to arrange that appointment;
  • Email and business-productivity providers — used to receive and review enquiries.

These providers process information on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own unrelated purposes.

What we do not do. Strategic Growth does not currently operate an automated CRM pipeline, automated email or automated telephone outreach, a website conversion pixel or dataset, or a Conversions API integration for this lead form. Any follow-up is performed manually. If we commission any of these in the future, we will update this policy before relying on them.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business and service records, after which we delete or anonymize it. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you provide, including restricting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take care to limit what we collect and who can access it.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have provided, or to stop contacting you. To make any of these requests, email strategicgrowth.biz@outlook.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also manage or withdraw lead-form permissions through your Meta account settings.

9. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
